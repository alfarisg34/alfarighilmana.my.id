# Design System: Alfari Sidnan Ghilmana Portfolio & Engineering Showcase

## Overview

**Aesthetic Direction: Industrial Telemetry & Editorial Precision (Anti-Template UI)**

This design system deliberately breaks away from homogenized AI templates (purple gradient mesh blobs, uniform bubbly `rounded-3xl` cards, generic Inter typography, and floating fake app cards).

Instead, it embodies the rigor of a senior systems engineer and architectural developer:
- **Atmospheric Depth**: Subtle noise texture + precision hairline gridlines (`1px solid rgba(...)`) + tactile top spotlighting.
- **Typography Pairing**: *Instrument Serif* (Display & Editorial italics) + *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Humanist body prose) + *JetBrains Mono* (Telemetry metrics, endpoints, and architectural maps).
- **Disciplined Geometry**: Sharp `0px–3px` chassis border-radii, crisp hairline rules, tactile status LEDs, and tabular numerical layouts.
- **Asymmetrical Split Cockpit**: Hero section with left-aligned editorial statement and right-aligned interactive **Live Systems Telemetry Console** monitoring active cluster nodes.
